Social Vision & Inclusivity

"Do not touch" is the rule of museums. We turned touch into the primary gallery key.

Traditional art spaces rely solely on visual sight. Chintamani Hasabnis pioneered tactile relief canvases incorporating 3D textures, varied natural grains, and Braille narrative scripts—opening art exhibitions to visually impaired art enthusiasts for the very first time.

Biography & Vision

Artist Statement

"My artistic foundation was built on rigorous realism and observation—capturing subtle light variations on human faces and exploring oil and canvas textures."

"As my studio practice evolved, I recognized a profound void in art galleries: millions of visually impaired people were excluded from visual storytelling. My tactile series is designed to eliminate this barrier—offering canvases that speak to sight and touch in equal measure."
